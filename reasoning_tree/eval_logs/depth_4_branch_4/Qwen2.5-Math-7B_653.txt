In Occidentalia, there are $20$ different companies, each looking to hire $15$ new employees. A group of $300$ applicants interview with each of the companies. Each company qualifies each applicant as suitable or not suitable to work in it, such that each company finds exactly $p$ suitable applicants, with $p > 15$. Furthermore, each applicant is found suitable by at least one company. What is the smallest value of $p$ for which it is always possible to assign $15$ applicants to each company, given that each company is assigned only applicants that it considers suitable, and that each of the $300$ applicants is assigned to a company?

Remember to put your answer in \boxed{...} format on its own line.
