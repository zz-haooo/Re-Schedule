- The second person enters, sees the first switch on, turns it off, and then lights the second bulb.

- The fourth person enters, switches off the first and second bulbs, and switches on the third.

This process continues in this manner. Finally, we find out that the first 10 bulbs are off and the 11th bulb is on. How many people were involved in the entire process?
